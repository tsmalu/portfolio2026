Capitães da Areia

Book design · Anhembi Morumbi University

Redesign of Jorge Amado’s novel Capitães da Areia (Captains of the Sands), developed for the Editorial Design course of my Graphic Design degree. The book measures 28 × 28 cm closed, a format chosen for a special edition.

The black, gray and yellow palette represents the contrast between the sand and the concrete of the city. The illustrations are my own, referencing Brazilian popular woodcut prints, and the typefaces simulate movable-type printing.
